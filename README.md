## The Hydrologic Unit (HU)
- The hydrologic unit (HU) is hirearchical system for standardizing the way data is collected, organized, mapped, and managed in the U.S. Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD).
- It follows nested pattern where each progressive hydrological unit is represented by an additional 2 digit code for successively smaller areal unit. 
- The smaller the number, the larger the area; the larger the number, the smaller the area
    - For example, starting with the largest area 18 and progressing to 1846852186, the smallest region: 18->1846->184685->184685->18468521->1846852186

## HUC-8
-  The WBD contains 8 nested levels of progressive hydrological units (2-16 digit codes)
- HUC-6 is completed (12 digit) for the United States and is necessary for U.S. coverage 
- USGS continues its survey to complete HUC-8 and for Mexico and Canada surveys
